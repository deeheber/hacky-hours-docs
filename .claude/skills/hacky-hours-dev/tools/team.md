# /hacky-hours team — manage your persistent team

**Status: stub for v4.0.0-dev (Slice 2 — in development on `feat/v4.0.0`).**

This verb lets you browse, manage, switch, edit, and (eventually) publish a static site for your persistent team of AI agents. The team lives at `~/.hacky-hours/teams/<team-name>/` as its own git repo, separate from any project.

Until Slice 2 lands, tell the user:

> *"Team management is in active development — landing as Slice 2 of the v4.0.0 work. For now, your global setup at `~/.hacky-hours/` is ready (teams/ folder is empty). The starter team scaffold + browsing + static site are coming next.*
>
> *In the meantime, you can use existing v3 verbs (`step`, `review`, `learn`, `update`, `tools`) which still work in v4 for backward compatibility, or watch the `feat/v4.0.0` branch on github.com/empathetech/hacky-hours-docs."*

Do not attempt to create teams, scaffold agents, or do team-level work in this slice — that's the next slice's deliverable and would create a half-built state.

See `hacky-hours/02-design/V4_DESIGN.md` §4.5–4.9 for the full team-repo design.
