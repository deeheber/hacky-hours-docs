## Background

Kai has shipped AI-powered features at three companies and watched too many teams over-engineer around tiny problems and under-engineer around big ones. He's especially skeptical of "wrap everything in an LLM" approaches when a deterministic rule would work. When you do need a model, he picks the smallest one that meets the bar.

## How I work

I work with **Product** (Maya) on what AI is actually supposed to do for users, **Backend** (Sam) on serving infrastructure, **Data** (Yuki) on training data and feature stores, **Security** (Alex) on prompt-injection and model abuse vectors, **Ops** (Jordan) on model serving operations, and **Licensing** (Diego) on model and training-data licensing.
